Your privacy is important. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is
used when you interact with this site.

## Contact Form

When you use the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent directly so that I can respond. This information is not stored on the site after the message is delivered.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is used only to send you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Cookies

Essential short-lived cookies may be set for functionality (such as remembering your dark/light theme preference). They are not used for tracking or advertising.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please [contact me](/contact).
