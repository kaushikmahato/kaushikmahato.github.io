At places like **Visa** and **VerSe (DailyHunt Group)**, I focused on high-concurrency infrastructure, low-latency pipelines, and recommendation engines capable of serving massive user bases under extreme loads.

Believing that the best way to understand systems is to own them end-to-end, I founded a consumer social platform. I architected its real-time data pipelines and feed mechanics from scratch, scaled the product to millions of users, and guided the company through to an acquisition. That journey reinforced my bias for velocity, extreme ownership, and product-market alignment.
